Patient Safety Mission

Empowering Medication Adherence

SmartMed bridges the critical gap between prescribed clinical therapy and daily patient adherence through intelligent scheduling, trustworthy medical education, and uncompromising data privacy.

Absolute Data Isolation

We adhere strictly to tenant separation principles. Each patient's medication list, medical records, schedules, and prescription attachments are encrypted and isolated so that no other user can access them.

Verified Public Health Sources

Our educational monograph catalog is compiled exclusively from verified public health authorities, including MedlinePlus (U.S. National Library of Medicine), NHS UK, and FDA official prescribing labels.

Active Multi-Device Reminders

With synthesized audio chimes, browser push notifications, and customizable time-of-day slots, patients never forget a critical morning, afternoon, or evening dose.

Architected for Healthcare Scale

SmartMed is built from the ground up without legacy PHP stacks or local XAMPP dependencies. The backend runs on high-performance Python FastAPI, asynchronous database connection pooling with SQLAlchemy and MySQL, and secure JWT token authentication.
